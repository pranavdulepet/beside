At the Bulandibagh dig

At Bulandibagh, excavators exposed surviving timbers from the wooden palisade that enclosed Pataliputra. The field report describes rows of heavy wooden sleepers set along the wall.
Reading: Archaeological Survey of India, Patna Circle, Bulandibagh (monument 48). J. A. Page, Annual Report 1926–27.
