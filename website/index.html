Beside

An assistant for any app on your Mac. It helps when you ask, steps in when useful, and builds personal context over time that you can take anywhere.

Download for Mac ↓
Install guide Privacy GitHub

Finds what you closed

Searches recent screens, Notes, Mail, and folders you pick so you don’t hunt for something you read earlier.

Helps in any app

Tap ⌥⇧Space to type, hold to speak, ask where to click on screen, or have a reply read aloud.

Steps in quietly

Spots a related note, an error, or an upcoming meeting when you pause typing, and backs off when you dismiss it.

Context you own

Saves what you teach it on your Mac in plain files you can inspect, delete anytime, or share selectively with other tools.

Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · Runs on your Mac
